📖 Effect Guide
Fisheye — Wide angle barrel distortion. 30-50% for GoPro look.
Superview — Stretches edges for ultra-wide feel.
Protune — GoPro's color science: punchy contrast, warm highlights.
Stabilize — Removes camera shake. Takes 2 passes — slow but worth it.
Boomerang — Video plays forward then reverses on loop.
Timelapse — Set to 10 to keep every 10th frame = 10x speed.
Burst — Extracts still frames from video. Good for thumbnails.
Wind Noise — Cuts low rumble. Essential for outdoor footage.
⚡ Speed Guide
• Normal export: ~2-5x slower
• Stabilize: ~4x slower (2 passes)
• Boomerang: ~3x slower
• Timelapse: faster (fewer frames)
• Burst: very fast
